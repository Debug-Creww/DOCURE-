01 Executive Problem Statement & The 2 AM Healthcare Crisis

Across urban and semi-urban populations, over 70% of households experience severe anxiety and delay when dealing with medical uncertainty. The traditional patient journey is broken across four critical friction points:

📑 1. Pathology Report Incomprehensibility

Diagnostic lab reports (CBC, Lipid, LFT, KFT) contain dozens of technical biomarkers (MCV, TIBC, SGOT, HbA1c). Patients resort to unguided internet searches, triggering unnecessary panic or ignoring genuine critical markers.

🏥 2. Emergency Location & Verification Friction

During sudden night emergencies, public search maps often display outdated numbers, closed clinics, or police stations instead of verified 24/7 trauma centers with direct emergency room lines.

💊 3. Medication Non-Adherence Epidemic

According to the WHO, 50% of chronic patients fail to take medicines correctly. Traditional reminder apps require manual alarm setups and lack cloud synchronization across caregiver devices.

⏱️ 4. Triage Delay Before Doctor Visit

Patients either over-triage mild conditions to crowded ERs or under-triage life-threatening cardiac symptoms, causing dangerous delays in emergency care.

💡 The DOCURE Thesis

DOCURE closes this complete loop: It extracts lab reports into plain-language clinical insights, maps verified local doctors & 24/7 pharmacies, enforces daily pill schedules via background daemons, and provides 1-tap emergency sirens with GPS dispatch.

03 Node-by-Node Technical Feature Breakdown

🔬 NODE 04: NVIDIA NIM AI Pathology & Lab Report OCR

Dual-Engine Architecture: If the patient uploads a digital PDF (e.g. Dr Lal PathLabs, SRL, Thyrocare), Mozilla PDF.js reconstructs lossless text coordinates directly in the browser and transmits them to Meta Llama 3.1 70B Instruct. If a camera photo is uploaded, Meta Llama 3.2 90B Vision parses the physical image.

PDF Upload → PDF.js Binary Extractor → Llama 3.1 70B (via /api/nvidia Proxy) → Balanced JSON Parser
Output: [Serial Test Rows] + [Unit & Range] + [HIGH/LOW Flags] + [Health Score: 0-100] + [Specialist Referral]

🧭 NODE 03: Real-Time Spatial Medical Radar & GIS

Integrates TomTom Search & POI API v2 with category codes: 7321 (Hospitals), 7322 (Doctors), 7324 (Clinics), and 7326 (Pharmacies). Renders hardware-accelerated Leaflet.js pulsing map pins with driving times, distance, and 1-click calling.

⏰ NODE 02: Medication Adherence Daemon

Cloud persistence powered by Firebase Firestore under users/{uid}/reminders with real-time websocket snapshot listeners. An autonomous 20-second polling daemon monitors medication schedules, generating Web Audio multi-frequency chimes and action toast overlays.

💬 NODE 01: Symptom Triage Chat

Multi-LLM triage desk with voice input (webkitSpeechRecognition) and speech synthesis (window.speechSynthesis) supporting Hindi & English.

🚨 NODE 05: Emergency SOS Siren

Generates dual-frequency 850Hz/650Hz siren waves using browser AudioContext.createOscillator() with 10s auto-dispatch.

06 Master Judge Q&A Defense Directory

Judge's Tough Question Winning Direct Technical & Clinical Answer
Q1: Is the AI diagnosing diseases? What about liability? "DOCURE does not diagnose. It performs triage, biomarker classification against printed normal reference ranges, and educational interpretation. Every report analysis directs the patient to the appropriate medical specialist."
Q2: Why use both 70B text and 90B vision models? "Digital lab PDFs contain structured text. Extracting text directly with PDF.js and feeding it to Llama 3.1 70B is 10x faster, cheaper, and 100% immune to image blur. 90B Vision is reserved for camera photos."
Q3: How do you prevent hallucinated biomarker values? "Our balanced JSON parser enforces sequential matching against the raw text vectors extracted by PDF.js. If a test value is not present in the source report, the AI is prompted to omit it rather than guess."
Q4: How does the background medicine daemon work if the app is minimized? "The notification scheduler runs an active 20-second polling loop coupled with Firestore snapshot listeners and supports 1-click sync with native Google Calendar alarms for system-level alerts."
Q5: How is patient data secured? "Lab reports are processed ephemerally through authenticated NVIDIA NIM endpoints without persistent public storage. Reminder documents are stored in user-isolated Firestore collections."
