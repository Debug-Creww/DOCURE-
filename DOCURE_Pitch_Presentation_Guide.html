DOCURE Pitch Presentation Script

Complete spoken pitch transcript, feature walkthrough flow, and judge Q&A defense strategy.

⚡ 1. The Hook & Problem Statement 0:00 - 0:45

"Good morning respected judges and everyone present here.

Let me start with a quick question: How many times have you or a family member received a blood test report, Googled the complex parameters at 2 AM, and panicked because the internet told you it's a critical disease?

Today, over 70% of people struggle with 3 major healthcare gaps:
  • Medical Jargon Overload: Complex pathology tests (like MCV, TIBC, WBC) that patients cannot interpret on their own.
  • Emergency Location Friction: Finding a verified, open hospital with a direct working contact takes too many clicks.
  • Medication Non-Compliance: Elderly and chronic patients consistently miss scheduled doses, causing preventable complications.
To solve this complete healthcare loop, we built DOCURE."

💡 2. Introducing DOCURE 0:45 - 1:15

"DOCURE is an intelligent, privacy-conscious Clinical Healthcare Assistant that connects patient symptoms, lab report diagnostics, verified local healthcare providers, and daily medication adherence — all within one cohesive, lightning-fast platform.

DOCURE is not just a chatbot. It is a full-fledged clinical intelligence desk powered by NVIDIA NIM AI, Meta Llama models, real-time spatial GIS, and autonomous background scheduling."

🖥️ 3. Core Product Walkthrough 1:15 - 3:00

🔬 1. NVIDIA AI Lab Report Analyzer

Users upload any blood test PDF or image. Mozilla PDF.js extracts lossless text and Meta Llama 70B & 90B Vision extract biomarkers, normal ranges, and flags HIGH/NORMAL/LOW with a clinical Health Score.

🧭 2. Real-Time Medical Radar

Queries TomTom POI API to plot live Hospitals, Doctors, Labs, and 24/7 Pharmacies on a Leaflet map with 1-Click Direct Calling and Google Maps turn-by-turn routing.

⏰ 3. Medication Reminder Daemon

Firestore-synced 20s polling listener that triggers in-app toast alerts, Web Audio chime synthesis, and 1-click Google Calendar alarm sync.

🚨 4. Emergency SOS & Siren

Synthesizes dual-frequency 850Hz ambulance siren in browser via Web Audio API and initiates a 10s auto-forward of GPS coordinates to family contacts.

🏗️ 4. Technical Architecture Highlights 3:00 - 3:45

"What makes DOCURE technically superior?
  • Dual-Engine AI Pipeline: Lossless text extraction with Meta Llama 3.1 70B for digital PDFs (fast & deterministic) + Llama 3.2 90B Vision for physical paper photos.
  • Zero-CORS Vite Proxy: Proxies requests to NVIDIA NIM with zero CORS overhead.
  • Browser-Native Audio: Zero third-party audio downloads — sirens and chimes are synthesized directly with Web Audio API oscillators.
"

🎯 6. Judge Q&A Cheat Sheet

Expected Judge Question Winning Direct Answer
Is the AI giving medical diagnoses? What about hallucinations? DOCURE does not diagnose. It performs triage, biomarker classification against standard printed reference ranges, and educational interpretation. It directs patients to verified specialists.
Why use both 70B text and 90B vision models? Digital lab PDFs (Dr Lal, SRL) contain structured text. Extracting text directly with PDF.js and feeding to 70B is 10x faster, cheaper, and 100% immune to image blur. 90B Vision is reserved for camera photos.
How is patient privacy protected? No patient images are permanently stored. AI analysis happens through secure enterprise NVIDIA NIM endpoints, and reminder data is stored in isolated Firestore user collections.
